## 5. Space estimate for the 60 formal samples

The formal samples are not started. Based on this round's measured artifacts:

| Component per sample | Measured / estimated |
|---|---|
| Trace v3.1 JSONL | 0.2–30 MB depending on event count (A03 ≈ 2 MB; replay workload ≈ 28 MB) |
| Propagation CSV (gzip) | 40 KB–5 MB |
| stdout/stderr/oracle/injection logs | < 2 MB |
| manifest/config/stats | < 1 MB |

A conservative budget is **20 MB/sample → 1.2 GB for 60 samples**, plus
**2–3 GB** of temporary CSV/log duplication during a batch, plus the final W1–W8
baselines (≈ 0.5 GB). Reserve **5 GB** for the formal campaign and keep the
current 29 GB free as the hard floor.

## 6. Recommended archive strategy (approval required)

1. **Keep** one canonical `gem5.opt` per active line (v3.2.2) plus its SHA256;
   archive the other seven binaries' identity (`sha256sum` + `git_commit`) and
   then drop the binaries only on approval. Reclaim ≈ 8 GB.
2. **Archive** (`tar.zst` + SHA256) the inactive R6 worktrees
   (`r6-p-unit-20261104`, `r6-c-unit-20261102`, `r6-s-unit-20261029`,
   `r6-o-unit-20261103`), keeping their manifests and reports. Reclaim ≈ 10 GB
   after compression, on approval.
3. **Keep** `runs/lsu` and all Trace evidence unmodified; it is the only copy of
   the R6/MDSPEC results.
4. Reclaim `runs/lsu` only after a verified external archive with checksums.
5. Do not run clean builds in parallel worktrees; each clean build temporarily
   needs several GB and the current 29 GB free is below a comfortable margin.

## 7. Risk statement

The 92 % usage is a **live risk** for the formal campaign: a clean gem5 build in
a new worktree plus 4 concurrent runs can exhaust the remaining 29 GB. No action
is taken here. The first two archive steps (≈ 18 GB) are recommended before any
60-sample campaign starts.
